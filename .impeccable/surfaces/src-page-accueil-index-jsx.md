# Surface brief: site du Ciné Action Festival

## Scope and mode

All routes of the React app (home, participer, à propos, FAQ, inscription, connexion, contact / à venir), redesigned in one world. Home, À propos and Participer are **Persuade**; FAQ and the rules blocks are **Read**; Inscription and Connexion are **Operate**.

## Audience, job, action

Filmmakers decide to enter and go to "S'inscrire au concours"; the audience understands the week and follows the festival. Real assets: Serge Crozon stunt showreel (hero), YouTube example film, festival description, five candidate steps. Everything else is provisional copy, flagged in the hand-off list. Craft bar: the Festival de Cannes website (institutional rigour, structured programme, restraint).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The festival site the category expects — a full-bleed stunt showreel, a massive condensed title, one red action — played straight at Cannes-level rigour. It refuses dragon, katana and brush-stroke kitsch, and template pages of centred text and lorem ipsum.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ground #0a0a0a, off-white type #f5f5f5, mid-greys for structure, a single red #d71e28 reserved for the primary action and live state. Anton uppercase for display, Archivo for text and UI. Square corners, 1px hairline rules, no shadows, no gradients except the video's legibility scrim.

STORY: In one viewport the visitor knows it is an action short-film festival with martial arts at its core, open to everyone, 2 min 30 on an imposed theme. Candidates take "S'inscrire au concours"; the audience takes "Découvrir le festival". Below: the week's four strands, the countdown, five steps, three crafts honoured, the reel, follow.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Muted showreel full-bleed with a bottom scrim. Top bar: logotype left, links, red "S'inscrire" right. Bottom-left: the title "CINÉ ACTION / FESTIVAL" at about 13vw (190px max) on two lines, one subline, red filled "S'inscrire au concours" and outline "Découvrir le festival". Bottom-right: four hairline boxes, the countdown to registrations.

FORM: canon (the standing exit, chosen by the user; not on the grounded list). seed:d184c97ae992856c. Signature: the title's line-by-line rise over the first frame of the reel; motion grammar is fast exponential ease-out reveals only.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
